Material Verification & Testing

Every batch of leather and raw materials undergoes strict evaluation before entering production. We ensure performance, durability, and visual consistency.

  • Leather thickness & grain inspection
  • Color consistency testing
  • Tensile strength & flexibility check
  • Hardware durability & rust resistance testing
  • Stitching thread strength evaluation

Production Stage Monitoring

Quality checks are conducted at multiple stages during production to prevent defects and ensure precision craftsmanship.

  • Pattern verification before cutting
  • Stitch alignment & seam strength inspection
  • Edge finishing & detailing accuracy
  • Branding & logo placement check
  • Dimensional measurement validation

100% Final Inspection Before Shipment

Each finished product is carefully reviewed to meet agreed specifications and buyer requirements before packaging and dispatch.

  • Visual defect screening
  • Functional testing (zippers, buttons, hardware)
  • Weight & size confirmation
  • Packaging compliance check
  • Export documentation verification
