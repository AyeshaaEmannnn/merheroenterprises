About Merchero Enterprises

Merchero Enterprises is a Pakistan–UK based leather manufacturing company specializing in performance motorbike racing suits and a wide range of premium leather products. Active in the industry since 2010, the company was founded by Muhammad Saad Madni and Muhammad Ahtisham Bahoo with the goal of building a reliable, market-ready manufacturing brand for global customers.

We began our journey by serving the national market and steadily expanded our operations to meet international manufacturing and quality expectations. To support global trade and partnerships, Merchero Enterprises was officially registered in the United Kingdom in 2024 and later registered in Pakistan in 2025. All production activities are carried out in Pakistan, where skilled craftsmanship is combined with structured manufacturing processes.
