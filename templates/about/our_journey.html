Our Timeline

2010

Foundation Through Practical Experience

Merchero Enterprises began its journey in 2010, driven by hands-on involvement in leather product development and motorbike riding gear.

One of our earliest milestones was manufacturing custom motorbike riding suits for Pakistani motorbike tourists who traveled internationally by bike. These products were designed to perform under real road conditions, climates, and long-distance usage.

Key Achievement: Early exposure to functional design, rider safety, and durability requirements.
2012–2018

National Manufacturing & Industry Collaboration

As our experience grew, we began working with multiple Pakistani brands and exporters as a manufacturing and support unit.

Leather jackets and gloves

Custom leather garments
Gained: Production skills, quality control practices, and OEM understanding.
2019–2023

Product Expansion & OEM Capability Building

With growing confidence and technical know-how, Merchero Enterprises expanded its focus beyond performance gear.

Developed: OEM and private-label capabilities including custom sizing, branding, sampling, and production planning.
2024

United Kingdom Registration

To strengthen our global presence and support international clients, Merchero Enterprises was officially registered in the United Kingdom in 2024.

This step allowed us to improve transparency, communication, and confidence for overseas buyers looking for a legally registered international partner.

Key Milestone: Transition from local manufacturer to globally oriented B2B partner.
2025

Pakistan Registration & Manufacturing Consolidation

In 2025, Merchero Enterprises was formally registered in Pakistan, consolidating our operations under a structured legal and manufacturing framework.

All production activities continue to be carried out in Pakistan, where skilled craftsmanship, controlled workflows, and cost-effective manufacturing come together.

Commitment: Long-term manufacturing stability, compliance, and scalable production.
