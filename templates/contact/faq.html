We provide full export documentation (Invoice, Packing List). Shipping costs are separate and handled by the client.

Incoterms

FOB means Merchero Enterprises delivers your goods to the Pakistan port and loads them onto the vessel. After this point, the buyer assumes responsibility for international freight, insurance, and customs clearance. Ideal for buyers who want control over shipping.

EXW allows buyers to collect goods directly from our factory in Sialkot, Pakistan. All transportation, shipping, customs clearance, and insurance responsibilities lie with the buyer. Perfect for clients with their own logistics teams.
