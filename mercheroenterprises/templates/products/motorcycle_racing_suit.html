Overview

Description

The Merchero Apex-R Elite is the flagship racing suit in our lineup, born from years of collaboration with professional motorcycle racers and safety engineers.

The outer shell utilizes a strategic combination of full-grain cowhide leather (1.2-1.4mm thickness) for structural integrity and kangaroo leather in high-flex zones for superior mobility.

Inside, a network of CE Level 2 protectors shields the most vulnerable impact zones. The suit's geometry is derived from 3D body scanning data to ensure a pre-curved, race-ready fit.

Safety

Protection System

CE Level 2 Armor

EN 1621-1 certified protectors at shoulders, elbows, and knees with dual-density foam absorption layers.

Titanium Sliders

Aerodynamic titanium shoulder and knee sliders reduce friction during slide impacts.

Back Protector Pocket

Dedicated internal compartment accepts CE Level 2 back protectors with secure Velcro retention.

Double Stitching

All critical stress seams feature double-row stitching with bonded nylon thread rated for 25kg tensile strength.

Chest Protector Ready

Internal pockets compatible with optional CE Level 1 or Level 2 chest protectors.

Boot & Glove Zippers

YKK zippers at calves connect directly to racing boots and gloves to prevent ride-up.

Construction

Material Breakdown

Cowhide Leather

Full-grain cowhide at 1.2-1.4mm thickness forms the primary shell for exceptional abrasion resistance and structural stability.

Coverage: ~75% of suit surface area

Kangaroo Leather

Premium kangaroo leather in high-flex zones. At equivalent weight, kangaroo leather offers up to 10x the tensile strength of cowhide.

Coverage: ~25% of suit surface area

Ergonomics

Comfort & Fit

The Apex-R Elite is engineered around the aggressive tuck position of sportbike riding. Through extensive 3D body-mapping and rider feedback, we've achieved a suit that feels natural on the bike — no pulling at the shoulders, no bunching at the waist, and no restriction at the knees.
